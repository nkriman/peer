[CRITICAL] pydantic/mypy.py:809
  `args[0]` will raise an `IndexError` if `args` is empty (e.g. a `RootModel` subclass that defines no fields, such as `Pets1` which inherits `root` from the parent but may not have it reflected in `fields`). Add a guard: `if is_root_model and args:` before indexing.
